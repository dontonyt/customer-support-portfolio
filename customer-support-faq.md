# Customer Support FAQ

## 1. How do I contact customer support?
Customers can contact the support team through the available support channels. Provide a clear description of the issue and any relevant details.

## 2. What should I do if I cannot access my account?
Confirm that your login details are correct. If the issue persists, document the error message and escalate it to the appropriate support team.

## 3. What if a payment or subscription issue occurs?
Confirm the details of the reported issue, document the relevant information, and escalate billing matters to the appropriate team when necessary.

## 4. How should a support request be handled?
Acknowledge the customer's concern, clarify the problem, investigate within your authority, explain the next steps, and follow up until the issue is resolved or appropriately escalated.

## 5. How should a technical issue be documented?
Record the issue, steps taken, expected and actual results, error messages, and any relevant evidence. Escalate reproducible or unresolved issues to the appropriate technical team.

## 6. How should an upset customer be handled?
Remain calm and professional, listen carefully, acknowledge the customer's frustration, explain realistic next steps, and avoid making promises that cannot be guaranteed.

## 7. When should an issue be escalated?
Escalate issues that exceed your access or authority, involve security or sensitive account concerns, or require technical intervention. Keep the customer informed throughout the process.

## 8. How can recurring customer issues be reduced?
Document recurring problems, identify patterns, share feedback with the relevant team, and update FAQs or troubleshooting guidance when an improvement is confirmed.

## Support Principles
- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Protect customer information.
- Keep accurate records.
- Take ownership and follow through.
- Escalate appropriately.
- Never invent answers or promise unconfirmed outcomes.
