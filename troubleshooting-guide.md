# Customer Support Troubleshooting Guide

## Scenario: A Customer Cannot Log In

### Step 1: Acknowledge the Issue
Respond politely, acknowledge the inconvenience, and reassure the customer that you will help investigate.

### Step 2: Gather Information
Ask the customer to describe the problem, including any error message and when the issue started. Never request their password or sensitive authentication details.

### Step 3: Check Basic Causes
- Confirm that the customer is using the correct login page.
- Ask them to verify their email address.
- Suggest using the password reset option if appropriate.
- If relevant, ask whether the problem occurs on another browser or device.

### Step 4: Document Findings
Record the reported issue, error message, troubleshooting steps, results, and any relevant timestamps in the support ticket.

### Step 5: Escalate When Necessary
If the issue persists, escalate it to the appropriate technical team with clear evidence and a summary of the steps already taken.

### Step 6: Follow Up
Keep the customer informed about the next steps. Confirm whether access has been restored before closing the ticket.

## Sample Customer Response

Hello,

Thank you for contacting support. I'm sorry you're having trouble accessing your account.

I'll help you work through the issue. Could you please share the error message you're seeing and confirm whether you've tried resetting your password?

Please do not send your password or other sensitive login information.

I'll review the details and guide you through the next steps.

Kind regards,  
Customer Support Team

## Key Principles
- Communicate with empathy.
- Troubleshoot systematically.
- Document accurately.
- Protect customer information.
- Escalate issues appropriately.
- Follow through until resolution.
